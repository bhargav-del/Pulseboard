# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and respect different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to **yuin39166@gmail.com**. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and useful.
